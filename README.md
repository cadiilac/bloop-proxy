<h1 align="center"> Bloop Proxy </h1>
<p align="center">Welcome to the Bloop Proxy repo, hosted by a friend, made to stay hidden from schools and other mainstream blockers.</p>
<p align="center">___</p>
<h3>About</h3>
<p>Bloop is a proxied browser made to support students during the hell we call school. We get it – school is boring as fuck: that's where Bloop comes in. Bloop is made to unblock all websites and be able to function as an entire unmonitored browser – aka. it's essentially a VPN. All Bloop does is reroute your location, making you unstoppable when it comes to mainstream blockers.</p>
